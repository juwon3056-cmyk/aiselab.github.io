About AISE Lab

Deep LearningStructural OptimizationComputer VisionComputational MechanicsConstruction RoboticsMetaheuristics

AISE Lab develops innovative AI-driven solutions for structural engineering challenges.

Our research bridges AI and structural engineering to create more efficient, reliable methodologies for structural analysis and design.

Led by Professor Seunghye Lee, our team explores deep learning surrogate models, structural optimization, and computational mechanics to address real-world engineering challenges.
