News

Lab announcements and activities

Aug 28, 2026

Participation in ETABS User Training

On August 28, 2026, our undergraduate researchers attended an ETABS user training session, working through the design of RC and steel structures and reviewing the KDS design features of the latest version. The session also introduced API automation supported by AI-assisted coding.

Jul 21, 2026

Workshop on Climate-Resilient Smart Reinforcement of Heritage Walls

On July 21–22, 2026, we took part in a research workshop in Buyeo on smart reinforcement and prediction technologies for heritage fortress walls against climate disasters, sharing research goals and exchanging ideas with the participating institutions.

Jul 20, 2026

Exchange with ETH Zürich Graduate Delegation

On July 20, 2026, a graduate delegation from ETH Zürich visited structural project sites as part of their study trip (Masterreise 2026). As a research partner, AISE Lab welcomed the delegation for a time of on-site exchange.

Dec 19, 2025

AISE Lab Founded

AISE Lab was officially established at Sejong University.
