Projects

Selected research and development projects

Ongoing · Participating

Sensor- and Image-Based Prediction and Early-Warning System

As a participating team in a research project on the safety of cultural-heritage structures, we are developing sensor- and image-based prediction models and an early-warning system.

Structural safety · Sensor & image-based prediction · Early-warning system

Ongoing

Robotic System for Efficient Structural Safety Inspection

We are developing a robotic system to automate and streamline the safety inspection of structures such as industrial facilities.

Construction & industrial robotics · Computer vision · Automated structural safety inspection

Completed

Structural Defect Detection with Generative Data Augmentation

For large structures with scarce defect data, we augment training data with generative AI and build a YOLO-based object detection model to automatically detect defects.

Computer vision · Generative data augmentation · YOLO object detection · Structural health monitoring

Completed

Unitree G1 Edu Plus Control & Operation Guide Development

We built the control and operation environment for the Unitree G1 Edu Plus humanoid robot, developed an operation guide, and held a technical seminar.

Humanoid robots · AI robot operation · Technical training
