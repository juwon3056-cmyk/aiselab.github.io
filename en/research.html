Research

Three core research directions of AISE Lab

Area 01

AI-based Structural Surrogate Analysis

We train neural networks on finite element analysis data to develop deep-learning surrogate models that efficiently approximate the results of complex structural analyses.

Deep learning for structural engineering · Computational mechanics & finite element analysis · Structural health monitoring · Data-driven decision making

Area 02

AI-based Automated Structural Design

We combine machine learning with metaheuristic optimization algorithms to automate the structural design process and derive optimal designs.

Generative design · Multi-objective structural optimization · Metaheuristic algorithms · Topology optimization

Area 03

AI-based Construction Robotics

We integrate computer vision, robotics, and AI to develop automation systems tailored to construction sites, with on-site safety and construction efficiency as core goals.

On-site monitoring vision systems · Safety & hazard detection · Automated progress tracking · Intelligent construction equipment operation
