AISE Lab

Research

We work in three directions: structural analysis, structural design, and construction sites.

Area 01

AI-based Structural Surrogate Analysis

We train neural networks on finite element analysis data to develop deep-learning surrogate models that approximate complex structural analysis results quickly and accurately.

Deep learning for structural engineeringComputational mechanics & finite element analysisStructural health monitoringData-driven decision making
Area 02

AI-based Automated Structural Design

We combine machine learning with metaheuristic optimization algorithms to automate the structural design process and derive optimal designs.

Generative designMulti-objective structural optimizationMetaheuristic algorithmsTopology optimization
Area 03

AI-based Construction Robotics

We integrate computer vision, robotics, and AI to develop automation systems tailored to construction sites, aiming to improve on-site safety and construction efficiency.

On-site monitoring vision systemsSafety & hazard detectionAutomated progress trackingIntelligent construction equipment operation
